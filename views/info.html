How It Works

How Driving Works

Carpool members take turns driving to the climbing gym in a fixed rotation. After each session is recorded, the rotation advances to the next driver in the list. When the last person in the rotation has driven, it wraps back around to the first.

The home page always shows who is next in the rotation so everyone knows who is driving before the session starts.

How Paying Works

Payments are tracked using a balance system to keep things fair over time. When someone pays for a round, their balance goes up by the number of other attendees. Each other attendee's balance goes down by one.

The member with the lowest balance is suggested as the next payer. This way, no one ends up paying significantly more than anyone else in the long run. Payment groups are tracked independently so each group stays fair on its own.

How Bitterballen Works

The bitterballen index is based on climbing performance during each session. As members climb harder grades and hit personal records, the index rises. You can track the current index on the Climbing page.

When the index reaches the target threshold, it triggers a bitterballen order for the group. After the order is placed, the index resets and the cycle starts again.

Recording a Session

Here is how to record a session step by step:

  1. Go to the Home page and check the attendance list to mark who is present.
  2. The app suggests who should pay (lowest balance) and who should drive (next in rotation).
  3. Confirm or adjust the suggestions if needed.
  4. Hit record to save the session. Balances update automatically and the driving rotation advances.

If a session was recorded by mistake, it can be deleted from the Stats page, which will reverse all balance changes.
